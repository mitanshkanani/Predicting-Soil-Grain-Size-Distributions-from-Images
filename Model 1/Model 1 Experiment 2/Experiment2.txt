15. DEVIATIONS FROM PLAN
   Factor A was implemented as FOUR rulers, not three: RES-MATCH was split into RES
   (device held fixed, sampling changed) and CAM+RES (both), so the anti-alias effect
   can be attributed separately from the colour-pipeline effect. Strictly more
   informative than planned; no model component changed.
   The plan's P5 assumed the ruler's bias factor was ~1.5x, taken from ad-hoc
   reconnaissance that averaged both camera directions. Measured properly in-cell the
   CAM+RES factor is 0.84x, so P5 is restated as 'land inside the
   printed band' rather than as a multiplier. Restated before the submission, not after.
   Ruler selection does NOT use the lowest nested score: the rulers evaluate different
   populations, so cross-ruler EMDs are not comparable. Selection is by the external-
   ordering test and then by deployment fidelity, stated in cell G1.
   No pretrained backbone, fine-tuning, attention pooling or self-supervised
   pretraining was introduced. The model is byte-for-byte Experiment 1's.
