MODEL 1 — EXPERIMENT 4 — DOES THE COLOUR BLOCK EARN ITS PLACE?
================================================================================

WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT REPRESENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One variable: how colour is REPRESENTED. Everything else is frozen at Experiment 3's
values — the texture core, the rank-3 curve basis, closed-form ridge, the monotone
projection, the two-stage tile->image->soil median aggregation, the 16 CV families, the
24/10 split, the EMD implementation, and the construction of the CAM+RES ruler.

It is also the first experiment whose central question is one the ruler is FORBIDDEN to
answer. See "Why this is not a formality" below.

WHY THIS IS THE NEXT EXPERIMENT — TWO SCHEDULED ONES WERE CANCELLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both cancellations are reproduced from artifacts inside the notebook (section D), not
asserted from a scratch file.

1. The scheduled E4, "train on resolution-matched views", is dead. Once the frequency
   features are removed the model is already blur-insensitive: across all nine
   train-blur x test-blur combinations the 12-feature set spans only 9.2 EMD, and matched
   training buys at most 2.3 on the deployment column. E3's feature selection absorbed
   the sharpness problem.

2. The scheduled aggregation experiment is dead. Adding mean, sd, p10 and p90 tile
   summaries took LOGO-CV from 41.15 to 42.22. Quintupling the feature space made it
   slightly worse. A grain-size DISTRIBUTION deserves a distributional summary in physics
   and not at n=24 labelled soils.

That leaves the camera's colour pipeline as the only measured, surviving source of domain
shift: colour moves 1.54 z between Motorola and Samsung, the largest of any family, and
the model still carries seven colour features.

WHY THIS IS NOT A FORMALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Experiment 3's own numbers cannot decide this:

  cell A  core only, no colour  ( 5 feats)  nested CAM+RES 56.98   nested CAM 49.27
  cell C  core + colour         (12 feats)  nested CAM+RES 51.87   nested CAM 52.26

CAM+RES prefers colour. CAM prefers no colour. The CAM+RES gap is 5 EMD, which is inside
the ~15 EMD that E3's ruling says may not be resolved. **The ruler is structurally unable
to make this choice, so it goes to the leaderboard.**

THE RULER'S STATUS, INHERITED FROM E3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Calibration against the four externally measured configurations:

  E1  CAM+RES 205.34 -> actual 172.70   factor 0.84
  E2  CAM+RES  75.73 -> actual  71.27   factor 0.94
  E3  CAM+RES  51.87 -> actual  61.24   factor 1.18

The factor moved monotonically outward and crossed its window. The RANKING held three
times running. The ruler's optimism GROWS as configurations improve — the signature of a
proxy that models the nuisances it was built from and goes blind once they are removed.

BINDING RULE: CAM+RES is an ORDINAL instrument. It may rank candidates. It must not
forecast a score, and it must not choose between two candidates whose readings differ by
less than ~15 EMD. Every expectation in this experiment is written as "better or worse
than 61.24", never as a number.

THE ARMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  texture core (always present, 5): e4, e8, e16, lum_sd, grad_mean

  C0  raw channel means + saturation + luminance percentiles   12 feats
      E3's selected configuration. Control; external score already known (61.24).
  C1  no colour                                                 5 feats
      The camera-preferred option. Maximum robustness and parsimony.
  C2a per-camera robust z-score of the same 7 colour feats     12 feats
      Device identity taken from the manifest. Removes the between-camera offset while
      keeping within-camera colour variation.
  C2b same, but device groups found by unsupervised clustering 12 feats
      Never reads the camera label. See "The two problems with C2".
  C3  chromaticity r=R/sum, g=G/sum replacing R,G,B; sat and    11 feats
      luminance percentiles kept
      Scale-invariant colour that cannot carry a device's gain or brightness.
      DEVIATION FROM PLAN, recorded: the plan listed C3 as 4 features, which would also
      have deleted the luminance percentiles and changed two things at once. Keeping
      luminance isolates the variable to the colour representation.
  C4  colour block alone, core removed                          7 feats
      NEGATIVE CONTROL. E3 measured colour-only transfer as catastrophic. If C4 does not
      reproduce that, the pipeline has changed and nothing else in this file is
      trustworthy.

No arm re-extracts anything. Every arm is derived from Experiment 3's existing tile
caches. C2 and C3 are new REPRESENTATIONS of an existing family, not new measurements.

THE TWO PROBLEMS WITH C2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 1 — circularity. Per-camera standardization removes precisely the between-camera
mean and scale difference that the CAM ruler measures. CAM would rate C2 near-perfect by
construction. This is the same failure mode that killed the resolution-matched training
idea, in our own experiment. Therefore:

  - C2 is never ranked on CAM alone.
  - C2 is ranked on CAM+RES, whose blur component is untouched by colour normalisation.
  - The circularity is demonstrated numerically in section I, not just asserted.
  - C2's true value is settled by the leaderboard.

Problem 2 — it may be a metadata exploit wearing a normalisation costume. C2a needs to
know which camera produced each test image. The delivered filenames do say, so it is
technically available, but the host varied cameras DELIBERATELY to force generalisation,
and the project rules already ban camera as an input feature. C2b exists to test this: if
clustering recovers the same grouping, the trick is legitimate and portable. If C2a beats
C2b by a wide margin, C2 only works by exploiting device identity and must be rejected on
principle even if it scores.

TRANSDUCTIVE NORMALISATION — A DECLARED DEVIATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project rule says feature standardisation is fitted on training folds only. C2's
per-group median/IQR are computed once per (view, camera) group over all rows of that
group, including the held-out soil's own rows, and reused across folds.

Justification: the transform is completely label-free, and the target view's statistics
are properties of unlabeled input data that the competition hands us. Recomputing per
fold was measured to be unnecessary for the ranking and would triple runtime.

This is a genuine, declared departure from the letter of the leakage rule. It is NOT
applied to any other arm. If it turns out to matter, C2's result is void and must be
re-run fold-wise.

EVALUATION CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CAM+RES (4, primary)  Mot>Samsung and Samsung>Mot, each against res14 and res16
  CAM      (2, secondary) both directions against the unblurred view
  LOGO-CV  (reported only, never used to choose)

Training views are always the unblurred real view, so CAM+RES holds blur OUT from the
training side — the ruler already tests generalisation to unseen sharpness.

STATISTICAL PROTOCOL — unchanged from E3 on purpose
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Nested procedure scores. Alpha chosen inside each outer fold on CAM+RES. The headline
   number is the score of the PROCEDURE, never of the best alpha.
2. Paired per-soil differences as the primary statistic.
3. The bootstrap resamples SOILS, not observations. The six conditions share 21 soils;
   treating them as independent would give falsely tight intervals. Asserted in-cell.
4. A null is reported as a null: "not distinguishable from zero", never "no effect".
5. THE ORDINAL RULE. If two arms' paired CI spans zero, the notebook must print
   "the ruler cannot separate these" and must not pick between them on score. The tie is
   broken by parsimony (fewer features) and the choice is flagged as a bet.

PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Written before the run. Arms C2, C3 and C4 were NOT tested in reconnaissance, so unlike
E3 most of these are genuinely blind. The blind/confirmation label is per prediction and
may not be edited afterwards.

  P1  C4 reproduces E3's catastrophic colour-only transfer, CAM > 75
      [CONFIRMATION — a pipeline consistency check, worth little if it passes]
  P2  C1 and C0 are NOT separable by CAM+RES: the paired CI spans zero
      [BLIND — this is the plan's central claim, so refuting it is the better news]
  P3  C3 transfers better than C0 under CAM+RES while retaining most of C0's in-domain
      score   [BLIND]
  P4  C2a beats C2b on CAM+RES, i.e. identity-based normalisation gains something
      clustering cannot recover   [BLIND]
  P5  the submitted arm scores below 61.24   [BLIND — the only real test]
  P6  no point estimate for the score is offered anywhere in this experiment; any
      forecast outside a +-15 EMD band is declared invalid by the ruling above
      [a commitment, not a prediction]

P2 is the important one. If the ruler CAN separate C0 from C1, then E3's ambiguous result
was a measurement problem rather than a power problem, and the ordinal-only ruling was
too hasty.

SELECTION AND SUBMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Rank arms by nested CAM+RES.
2. Apply the ordinal rule; unresolved ties go to fewer features.
3. C2a is excluded from internal ranking entirely as the circular arm. It is submitted
   only if it wins on CAM+RES, and the record must state that basis explicitly.
4. The record must state which of two reasons applies to the chosen arm: either the ruler
   separated the arms, or it did not and parsimony decided.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - P2 refuted (C0 and C1 separate cleanly) -> the ruler has more power than E3's ruling
    allowed; revisit the ordinal constraint.
  - No arm separates from C0 -> Model 1 has plateaued. Sharpness handled, aggregation
    exhausted, colour undecided. Model 2 becomes an evidence-backed decision, not
    restlessness. This is a legitimate and expected outcome, not a failure.
  - C2a >> C2b -> reject C2 on principle regardless of score.
  - C4 fails to reproduce E3 -> stop everything; the pipeline drifted.

LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - 21 dual-camera soils. Six conditions raise the observation count but not the number
    of independent soils. This design cannot resolve differences below ~15 EMD. That is a
    property of the dataset, not of the design.
  - Five arms is more multiple-comparison surface than E3's two factors. The ordinal rule
    is the only guard and it must be applied, not admired.
  - The synthetic resolution camera is a Gaussian approximation of a Lanczos prefilter.
  - Public leaderboard is 3 fixed soils of 10; final score is 0.30*public + 0.70*private.
  - All four calibration points assume the public subset never changed. That is the
    largest single assumption in the project and it is untestable from our side.

RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Never overwrite a previous experiment's results. E1, E2 and E3 are read-only inputs.
A failed experiment is still research information.
