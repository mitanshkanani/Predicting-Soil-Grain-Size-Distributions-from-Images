11b. THE HEADLINE RESULT OF EXPERIMENT 4
   The colour question is answered: colour earns its place. Dropping it cost +15.89
   EMD externally, and CAM+RES's preference for keeping it was right.
   But the larger finding is about the instrument. Tested on all four externally
   measured configurations, 1 of 6 pairs are ranked backwards by
   CAM+RES:
     E2 vs E4: ruler says E2 is 18.75 better; reality says E2 is 5.85 worse
   E3's ruling said CAM+RES 'may rank, may not forecast'. E4 falsifies the ranking
   half as well. The ruler is not valid across representational families; it appears
   specifically to under-penalise REMOVING colour, which is the one thing E4 was built
   to test. That is not a coincidence: the ruler's only two colour-relevant nuisances
   are the Motorola-Samsung offset and a Gaussian blur, and neither reproduces
   whatever makes colour informative on the iPhones.
   CONSEQUENCE: no further Model 1 experiment may be selected by CAM+RES alone. Any
   future internal ranking must be treated as a hypothesis to be tested by submission,
   and submissions must be spent on well-separated candidates rather than fine
   distinctions.
