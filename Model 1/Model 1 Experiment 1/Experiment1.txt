   (d) FOLLOW-UP ANALYSIS (read-only, local, 2026-09-27; not part of Experiment 1):
       Mechanism behind the 172.70 score, found by comparing the test soils' feature
       vectors against the training envelope. Measured as the excess beyond the training
       [min,max], normalised by the training range, so it cannot be inflated by a
       near-constant column:
         texture features (7) : 1 of 70 test cells outside, excess 0.00 x range
         colour/geometry  (9) : 35 of 90 outside
         all 16, dropping the degenerate column below: 27 of 140 outside, mean 0.17 x range
       Every out-of-domain cell is carried by colour or geometry. The texture bands,
       gradients and frequency features land INSIDE the training envelope on cameras the
       model has never seen, which is direct evidence that the canonical-PPM and
       P3-to-sRGB normalisation did their job for texture.
       BUG FOUND: soil_fraction is constant to 4 decimal places across all 24 training
       soils (mean 0.9999, std 0.00027, range 0.0012) but ranges down to 0.829 on the test
       soils. Its z-score is therefore a divide-by-noise amplifier and it is unlearnable
       in-domain while varying out-of-domain. It must be dropped from every Model 1
       experiment. crop_area_fraction is the same failure in milder form (8 of 10 test
       soils outside, 0.89 x range). B and sat are outside on 8 of 10 each.
       59% of predicted test columns sit pinned at exactly 0 or 100, against 27% in real
       labels, and 8 of 10 rows reach 100 by the 0.63 mm point.
       Alpha sweep on the identical matrix (LOGO cv | test saturation | test vs mean curve):
         0.03 (Exp1 choice) -> 36.03 | 59.1% |  92.8
            1              -> 40.77 | 30.9% |  77.1
           10              -> 47.15 | 22.7% |  64.4
          100              -> 65.07 | 17.3% |  44.7
         1000              -> 87.32 | 16.4% |  22.2   (cv now WORSE than no-image)
        10000              -> 92.31 |  9.1% |   4.6   (model has become the mean curve)
       CV improves monotonically as alpha falls, so CV selects alpha=0.03, which is optimal
       in-domain and catastrophic out-of-domain. The selection criterion, not the regressor,
       is the broken component. This must be fixed before any further Model 1 experiment is
       scored, and before any backbone is fine-tuned, or every future conclusion is void.
       CALIBRATION OF THE INTERNAL PROXY: on the 21 soils photographed by BOTH training
       cameras, the Motorola-vs-Samsung feature shift for the same soil averages 0.81
       training ranges of |z| and never exceeds 2.65. The train-to-test shift is far larger.
       A held-out-camera test inside training is therefore a LOWER BOUND on the real gap,
       not an estimate of it.
