12b. WHAT THE BLIND PREDICTIONS DECIDED ABOUT THE RULER
   calibration across the three externally-measured configurations:
     E1  CAM+RES 205.34 -> actual  172.70   factor 0.84
     E2  CAM+RES  75.73 -> actual   71.27   factor 0.94
     E3  CAM+RES   51.87 -> actual   61.24   factor 1.18
   The factor has moved monotonically OUTWARD (0.84 -> 0.94 -> 1.18) and crossed the
   P6 window. So the ruler's absolute calibration is NOT stable and P5/P6 are void.
   The RANKING, however, held for the third consecutive time: the ruler put E1 worst,
   E2 middle, E3 best, and so did Kaggle. P4 is the confirmation of that.
   Marginal check: the ruler said E2->E3 was worth 23.9 EMD; it was worth 10.0. Its
   optimism GROWS as configurations improve -- the signature of a proxy that models
   the nuisances it was built from and goes blind once those are removed.
   RULING, binding from E4 onward: CAM+RES is an ORDINAL instrument. It may rank
   candidates. It must not forecast a score, and it must not choose between two
   candidates whose readings differ by less than ~15 EMD.

16. LIMITS
   21 dual-camera soils. Four conditions raise the observation count but not
   the number of independent soils, so the power ceiling is the 21.
   This design cannot resolve differences much below ~15 EMD; the colour null is an
   absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

17. KNOWN COST OF THIS EXPERIMENT
   Removing the frequency family removes dom_wavelength_mm, the only feature whose
   units are millimetres of actual soil texture and the only one a soil scientist can
   read directly. The model becomes less interpretable even if it scores better.
   Restoring that with blur-robust band RATIOS (e4/e8) rather than absolute spectral
   position is E5's job.

18. DEVIATIONS FROM PLAN AND MID-RUN CORRECTIONS
   (a) Reconnaissance (scratch/cal_exp3.py) ran BEFORE the plan was written and already
       answers P1-P3, so those are labelled confirmations rather than blind tests.
       Only P4-P6 are genuinely blind. Nothing was re-labelled after the fact.
   (b) Factor A was implemented as SIX conditions (2 camera directions x 3 views), not
       the four the plan listed: the two extra 'real' conditions are the CAM ruler used
       by the minimax check. Strictly more informative; no model component changed.
   (c) BUG FOUND AND FIXED, first run. The E5 reproduction assertion FAILED at a drift
       of 2.18e+01. Cause: E3 was aggregating tile -> soil x camera -> soil (pooling
       cameras before soils) while E1/E2 aggregate tile -> image -> soil (pooling all
       images). The assertion caught a silent incompatibility that would have made
       every number in this experiment meaningless. Fixed; drift is now 2.8e-14.
   (d) BUG FOUND AND FIXED, before any conclusion was drawn. The first implementation
       of the interaction test computed (frequency main effect) minus (colour main
       effect), which is large by construction whenever one factor matters and the
       other does not -- it is not an interaction. It reported P3 as REFUTED. The
       correct 2x2 interaction, (B-A) - (D-C), gives -1.64 CI [-6.63,+3.17] and P3
       CONFIRMED. The wrong version was never submitted anywhere; it was caught in
       the run log. Recorded here because a corrected statistic must stay visible.
   (e) A PLAN PREDICTION THAT DID NOT COME TRUE. model_plan_exp3.md section 5 said 'I
       expect rule 3 to fire', i.e. that primary and minimax would disagree. They
       agreed on cell C. The disagreement seen in reconnaissance (CAM preferring the
       5-feature core) was an artefact of comparing each cell at its OWN oracle alpha;
       under a common nested procedure it disappears. Lesson: oracle-alpha comparisons
       across feature sets manufacture differences that are not there.
   No pretrained backbone, fine-tuning, attention pooling, new features, or change to
   aggregation, output representation or estimator was introduced.

19. NEXT — REVISED AFTER THE SCORE, NOT AFTER THE INTERNAL RESULT
   The scheduled E4 (train on resolution-matched views) is CANCELLED WITH EVIDENCE.
   Once the frequency features are gone the model is already blur-insensitive: across
   all nine train-blur x test-blur combinations the 12-feature set spans only 9.2 EMD,
   and matched training buys at most 2.3 on the deployment column. E3's feature
   selection absorbed the sharpness problem, so there is nothing left for E4 to fix.
   The scheduled aggregation experiment is ALSO cancelled: adding mean, sd, p10 and
   p90 summaries to the tile aggregation took LOGO-CV from 41.15 to 42.22. Quintupling
   the feature space made it slightly worse.
   A THIRD finding, and the most transferable one: training on a blurred view and
   scoring with a ruler whose target is that same blurred view is CIRCULAR. Under that
   comparison the frequency features E3 removed look HELPFUL (-3.08 EMD), a 34-EMD
   artefact. Under held-out blur they still cost +35 to +58. Rule established:
   a treatment that alters the training distribution must be evaluated on a held-out
   level of the nuisance it targets.
   E4 becomes: does the colour block earn its place? CAM prefers no colour (49.27 vs
   52.26); CAM+RES prefers colour (51.87 vs 56.98). The gap is 5 EMD, inside the ~15
   EMD the ordinal ruling in 12b says we may not resolve. That is precisely the
   decision the ruler is now forbidden to make, so it goes to the leaderboard.
