MODEL 1 — EXPERIMENT 3 — FEATURE-FAMILY FACTORIAL (colour x frequency)
================================================================================

WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT REPRESENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E3 is the first ordinary Model 1 experiment: ONE variable changes — the feature set —
and it is judged by the selection ruler that Experiment 2 validated.

It is also the first genuinely out-of-sample test of that ruler. E2 calibrated CAM+RES on
a configuration change that altered regularisation strength and removed two degenerate
columns. E3 changes which feature FAMILIES exist. That is a different axis, and the ruler
may not transfer to it. Whether it does is the experiment's real question.

WHAT IS FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything except the feature set:
  ruler            CAM+RES, established by E2 (see Experiment2.txt section 10)
  output           rank-3 PCA curve basis + clip/cummax/force-100 monotone projection
  estimator        closed-form ridge, alpha swept 0.03 .. 1000, never hand-picked
  aggregation      tile -> image median -> soil median (two-stage), soil_fraction >= 0.5
  validation       the 16 curve-distance families, copied from E1/E2 unchanged
  split            24 train / 10 test soils
  metric           trapezoid EMD on log10 d, reconciled against the host's 100.31

THE DESIGN — a 2 x 2 factorial on a fixed texture core
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  texture core (5)  e4, e8, e16, lum_sd, grad_mean
  colour      (7)  R, G, B, sat, lum_p10, lum_p50, lum_p90
  frequency   (2)  spec_centroid_cpm, dom_wavelength_mm

  cell A  core                        5 features
  cell B  core + frequency            7
  cell C  core + colour              12
  cell D  core + colour + frequency  14   <- identical to Experiment 2's configuration

Neighbouring cells differ by exactly one family, so every simple effect is a
single-variable comparison and the two main effects are orthogonal by construction.

WHY THIS DESIGN AND NOT A BIG SWEEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A 64-combination family lattice would produce 64 numbers whose CIs are +-20 EMD wide,
and the minimum of 64 noisy numbers is noise. Four pre-specified cells with paired
statistics is a design; a sweep is a fishing trip.

STATISTICAL PROTOCOL — the part that must not be shortcut
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PAIRED DIFFERENCES ARE THE PRIMARY STATISTIC. Every comparison is reported as the mean
   per-soil difference with a bootstrap CI and a sign count ("wins 7/21"). Unpaired means
   are secondary and labelled as such.
2. FOUR EVALUATION CONDITIONS, not one: {Motorola->Samsung, Samsung->Motorola} x
   {resolution target iPhone 14, iPhone 16}. This raises the observation count from 21 to
   84.
3. THE BOOTSTRAP RESAMPLES SOILS, NOT OBSERVATIONS. The four conditions share soils, so
   resampling observations would treat correlated measurements as independent and produce
   falsely tight CIs. This is the single most important implementation detail in the
   notebook and it is asserted.
4. A NULL IS REPORTED AS A NULL. Where a CI spans zero the record says "not
   distinguishable from zero", never "no effect". Absence of evidence is not evidence of
   absence, and this design cannot resolve effects much below ~15 EMD.
5. MULTIPLICITY. 4 cells x 4 conditions = 16 simple-effect tests. All are reported, but
   the conclusion rests on the two MAIN EFFECTS, each averaged over its two levels.
6. CIRCULARITY. Alpha is chosen inside each outer fold; the headline number is the score
   of the nested PROCEDURE, never of the best alpha.
7. LEAKAGE. Scaler and curve basis refit per training fold. Under any camera ruler the
   held-out soil's label never enters the fit even though that soil exists under the
   other camera. Asserted in-cell.

SELECTION RULE — stated before the run
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. primary pick = cell with the best nested CAM+RES procedure score
  2. robustness check = minimax pick, the cell minimising its worst score across CAM and
     CAM+RES. If primary and minimax disagree AND the paired CI between them excludes
     zero, submit the cell with FEWER features.
  3. if the winner's advantage over cell D (E2's configuration) has a CI spanning zero,
     the submission is labelled "a bet, not a finding" in Experiment3.txt.

PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Written before the run, from reconnaissance in scratch/cal_exp3.py. That reconnaissance
already answered P1-P3, so THEY ARE CONFIRMATIONS, NOT BLIND TESTS, and passing them is
worth little. Only P4-P6 are genuinely blind. This is stated here so the record cannot be
read back as foresight.

  P1  the frequency main effect under CAM+RES is positive (with-frequency worse) with a
      soil-bootstrap CI excluding zero in >= 3 of 4 conditions        [confirmation]
  P2  the colour main effect under CAM+RES has a CI spanning zero     [confirmation]
  P3  no colour x frequency interaction (main effects ~additive)      [confirmation]
  P4  the E3 submission scores below E2's 71.27                       [BLIND]
  P5  the actual score lands in 45-52                                 [BLIND]
  P6  the implied calibration factor stays within 0.7-1.1             [BLIND]

P4-P6 are the experiment. P1-P3 are its plumbing.

KNOWN COST OF THIS EXPERIMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Removing the frequency features removes `dom_wavelength_mm`, the only feature whose units
are millimetres of actual soil texture and therefore the only one a soil scientist can
read directly. The model becomes less interpretable even if it scores better. Restoring
that interpretability with blur-robust band RATIOS (e4/e8 rather than absolute spectral
position) is E5's job, and the record says so rather than celebrating the simplification.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - If P4 is REFUTED while P1-P3 hold, the ruler ranks feature-family changes but cannot
    predict them. It must then be demoted from a selection device to a diagnostic, and
    Model 2 must not be scoped against it.
  - If the primary and minimax picks disagree, colour is doing something the CAM+RES
    average hides, and E4 must test colour under a sharper blur model.
  - If the frequency effect is significant under RES but not under CAM, the mechanism is
    confirmed as a sharpness artefact rather than a soil-signal artefact, which is the
    outcome reconnaissance predicts.

LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - 21 dual-camera soils. The four conditions raise the observation count but not the
    number of independent soils; the ceiling on this design's power is the 21.
  - The synthetic resolution camera is a Gaussian approximation of a Lanczos prefilter.
    If the real iPhone pipeline differs more sharply than that, colour's apparent
    harmlessness is an artefact of the model, not a property of the features.
  - Public leaderboard is 3 fixed soils of 10 and the final score is 0.30*public +
    0.70*private. One submission is a weak measurement, not a verdict.

RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Never overwrite a previous experiment's results. E1's and E2's folders are read-only
inputs to this experiment. A failed experiment is still research information.
