MODEL 6 / APPROACH A - ATTENUATION CORRECTION OF PREDICTED CURVES
================================================================================
Status: SPECIFICATION FOR OWNER REVIEW. No code exists. No experiment has been
run. Nothing in this folder is evidence of a result.

This is a NEW model. Models 1, 2 and 5 are read-only inputs. Their notebooks,
CSVs, records, thresholds and verdicts are never edited by anything in this
folder.


WHAT APPROACH A IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every model this project has run changed the INPUTS: hand features (M1), a
pretrained backbone (M2), an input-distribution alignment (M5). Approach A
changes exactly one thing downstream of the inputs: it rescales the predicted
curve away from the training centroid,

    y_hat' = y_bar + k * (y_hat - y_bar)

where y_bar is the fitted training mean curve and k >= 1 is a single scalar
estimated ONLY from inner folds. Features, head, ridge, alpha, rank-3 basis,
preprocessing and the CDF projection all stay byte-frozen.

The mechanism on offer is attenuation: a shrunk linear head systematically
under-predicts how far a soil's curve sits from the training mean, and if the
Kaggle soils sit further out than the training soils do, every prediction is
pulled too far inward and EMD punishes that directly.

k is estimated by a 1-D parsimony search over a frozen grid on inner folds, in
the same spirit as the project's existing ridge-alpha parsimony rule. It is
NEVER chosen by looking at the fold being scored, and NEVER chosen by looking
at Kaggle.


READ THIS BEFORE APPROVING IMPLEMENTATION - the premise test already ran
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 2.3 records pre-registration probes run on 2026-09-29 while writing this
spec. Their direction is UNFAVOURABLE to Approach A, and three of them are
decisive against the way I first got excited about it:

  1. The probe that motivated this whole approach (expansion taking an
     extrapolation score from 50.96 to 35.42) was an ORACLE-k result: one free
     scalar fitted to the six points being scored. Measured against the correct
     null - tuning any one-parameter family on six random soils - that gain sits
     inside the chance band (median +3.82, p90 +11.71, max +40.18 EMD). It is
     not evidence.
  2. The asymmetry that made the mechanism look real does NOT survive a
     deployment-shaped split. Expansion should help when scoring displaced soils
     and hurt when scoring interior soils. Measured with displacement defined in
     FEATURE space, both ends gain: +6.61 (most displaced) and +7.43 (least
     displaced). A correction that helps in both directions is not correcting
     displacement.
  3. The +16.98 zero-skill external gap is real arithmetic, but it does NOT
     establish range displacement. It is close to a tautology: a baseline
     computed as the mean of the training soils must score worse on soils it was
     not computed from. It cannot be used to infer where the test soils sit.

What survives: the Kaggle test set IS displaced in feature space (35/90 colour
and geometry cells outside the training envelope; 60% of test soils have a cell
beyond 3 SD; displacement ratio 1.46x by the trustworthy Euclidean measure).
But its predicted curves are 0.81x as far from the training mean as the
training soils' own, in a direction the head maps with LOW gain. That is not
the high-gain overshoot this correction assumes.

OWNER CHOICE, and the reason this spec is written before code:
  (a) Run Approach A anyway, as a CHEAP REGISTERED CLOSURE. The gate below is
      designed so that a pass requires clearing the one-parameter null, and
      every probe says it will not. Cost is one build plus a minutes-long CPU
      run. Benefit: a documented, pre-registered, null-anchored refutation that
      closes attenuation properly instead of on a vibe, and the null instrument
      becomes reusable for every future model.
  (b) Abandon Approach A now, before implementation, and move to M6-B (feature
      containment) or M6-C (ensembling), neither of which has been measured
      against a one-parameter null yet.
  (c) Redefine the correction so it is not a single global scalar - the evidence
      points at direction-dependent gain, which is a different and larger idea
      and would need its own brainstorming pass.

My recommendation is (a) if the run can stay under an hour of build effort,
otherwise (b). It is not (a) because the hypothesis looks likely; it is (a)
because the gate is now cheap, honest and reusable, and because a documented
closure on the one term worth 16.98 EMD is worth more than an unregistered
"we tried it in chat".


THE OBJECTIVE, AND WHAT WOULD SATISFY IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Approach A exists to produce a legitimate Kaggle submission or nothing. Legiti-
mate means: the decision to submit is made by a rule written in this file, from
labelled training data only, before any Kaggle score is seen.

  - At most ONE production submission from Approach A.
  - Zero submissions if the gate fails. A failed gate is written up and closed;
    it does not get a "just to see" upload.
  - No threshold, no k, no arm and no grid may be changed after a Kaggle score
    exists.


THE FROZEN CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inherited unchanged from Models 1, 2 and 5:
  - data/Training, data/Test, data/processed_meta are read-only. No re-pre-
    processing. config_hash must remain 010f44c36c74.
  - 12 control features, tile -> image median -> soil median aggregation,
    StandardScaler, rank-3 curve basis, closed-form ridge, the 16-value alpha
    grid, monotone CDF projection, trapezoid EMD on log10 d.
  - Camera identity, ppm, EXIF, ICC profile and sample id are not model inputs
    and are never dispatch keys. They may be used to BUILD A VALIDATION SPLIT,
    because a split is not a predictor, but never to transform a prediction.
  - CAM+RES is ordinal-only: it may rank, it may never forecast.
  - The public leaderboard is a fixed 3 of 10 test soils; final ranking is
    0.30*public + 0.70*private. A public difference of a few EMD is not evidence.
  - No model is ever selected by leaderboard movement.
  - ASCII-only printed output. No mock mode. Every cell must print something.

Changed by Approach A, and only this:
  - One expansion step applied to the predicted curve, between the rank-3
    reconstruction and the monotone projection.


ARMS - FIVE, AND WHY EACH EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A0  k = 1.000            the frozen pipeline exactly. The identity control.
                           Any difference from published M1/M2 numbers at k=1 is
                           an implementation defect, not a result.
  A1  k = k_hat (nested)   THE CANDIDATE. k_hat chosen by the frozen parsimony
                           rule on inner folds of the scored configuration only.
  A2  k = k_oracle         argmin over the grid on the scored fold itself. DIAG-
                           NOSTIC ONLY, never submittable, never gate-relevant.
                           Its only job is to be compared against the null in
                           N4 below. Conflating A2 with A1 is the exact error
                           that produced the false hope in section 2.3.
  A3  best alpha, k = 1    the "it is just regularisation" control. Full grid
                           sweep at k=1, best by the same inner-fold rule.
  A4  direction placebo    expansion of the same magnitude along a random
                           rank-3 direction instead of the prediction's own.
                           Must be able to differ; if it matches A1, the gain is
                           metric geometry and not attenuation.

k grid, frozen: 1.00 1.05 1.10 1.15 1.20 1.30 1.40 1.50 1.60 1.80 2.00
Parsimony rule, frozen: smallest k whose inner-fold mean EMD is within 2% of the
inner-fold minimum. Same shape as the existing ridge rule, so it can be audited
against a rule the project already trusts.


VALIDATION - THREE RULERS, AND THE NULL THAT MAKES THEM MEANINGFUL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N1  FEATURE-DISPLACEMENT HOLDOUT  (primary; deployment-shaped)
    Rank the 24 labelled soils by label-free distance from the training feature
    centroid. Score the most-displaced h, fit on the rest, estimate k_hat on
    inner folds drawn from the fit set only. Repeat for h in {6, 8, 10} and over
    multiple interior splits. This is the ruler that resembles deployment: the
    scored soils are genuinely outside the fit set's feature support.
    Note explicitly why this replaced the label-displacement split: soils with
    extreme D50 turn out to be un-displaced in feature space (mean distance 3.35
    vs 3.35 for the corpus), so extrapolating in LABEL space tests a condition
    the Kaggle set does not exhibit.

N2  FAMILY HOLDOUT  (secondary; the historical ruler)
    The existing 16-fold CV-family leave-group-out. Reported for continuity with
    Models 1-5 and to show how much the old ruler hides. It is INTERPOLATION and
    is not sufficient to authorise a submission on its own.

N3  REVERSE-DISPLACEMENT HOLDOUT  (falsification control)
    N1 inverted: fit on the most-displaced soils, score the least-displaced.
    If expansion helps here as much as in N1, the attenuation story is wrong and
    the experiment must be reported as evidence AGAINST Approach A regardless of
    what N1 says. The probes already show this happening (+6.61 vs +7.43), which
    is the single most important number in this file.

N4  ONE-PARAMETER NULL  (the gate's anchor; new to this project)
    Distribution of the A2-style gain obtained by tuning any single scalar on h
    random held-out soils, h in {6, 8, 10}, over >= 2000 draws. Every claimed
    gain must be compared to this, not to zero. This instrument did not exist
    when the earlier models were gated, and it is the reason the motivating
    result evaporated.

Uncertainty: paired per-soil differences, soil-clustered bootstrap, 4000 draws,
seed fixed below, resampling unit = the soil. Never an unpaired comparison of
means.

Seeds, fixed now: EXPLORE_PROBE 20260939 (the section 2.3 probes, already run),
M6A_SEED 20260940, M6A_BOOT 20260941, M6A_NULL 20260942, M6A_PLACEBO 20260943.
Head and alpha use Model 2 E3's frozen pipeline, which is itself seed-pinned at
20260930 by its cache filenames.


GATE - CLAUSE BY CLAUSE, WITH THE THRESHOLDS AND WHERE THEY COME FROM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G0  IDENTITY       A0 reproduces Model 2 E3's recorded 43.0217308796477 within
                   0.05 EMD under N2. Read from E3's sweep_results.csv at run
                   time, never typed. Failure means Approach A is not running on
                   the frozen head and NOTHING else is evaluated.
                   0.05 is the project's standing G0 tolerance from M5, kept
                   identical so the two experiments' sanity bars are the same.
G1  NESTED GAIN    A1's paired gain over A0 on N1 clears the N4 null: it must
                   exceed the null's 97.5th percentile for the same h, and its
                   own soil-clustered CI must exclude zero.
                   There is deliberately no fixed EMD floor. Every previous
                   floor in this project was anchored to a comparison against
                   zero, and N4 shows the real comparison is against what
                   free parameters buy at n=6.
G2  NOT-REGULARISE A1's gain over A0 exceeds A3's gain over A0 by at least 1.00
                   EMD on N1. 1.00 EMD is the project's standing smallest-signal
                   unit: it is the margin used by M5's G2, it is well above the
                   0.674 EMD public difference formally declared a tie, and below
                   it two configurations are not distinguishable here.
G3  PLACEBO        A1 beats A4 on N1 by at least 1.00 EMD with CI excluding zero.
G4  SPECIFICITY    A1's N1 gain strictly exceeds A1's N3 gain. This is the
                   attenuation direction test and it is a PASS/FAIL, not a
                   diagnostic. It fails on current evidence.
G5  STABILITY      k_hat's spread across N1 folds is reported, and at least 75%
                   of folds select k_hat >= 1.10. A correction picked by half the
                   folds is not a correction.
G6  BOUNDARY       k_hat strictly inside the grid. If it lands on 1.00 the result
                   is NO EFFECT, which is REFUTED, not a boundary hit. If it
                   lands on 2.00 it is INCONCLUSIVE, because the useful range was
                   truncated. Reported per ruler, never pooled - the M5 5a rule.
G7  LABEL-BLIND    No test label exists or is read; k_hat touches inner training
                   folds only; camera, ppm, EXIF, ICC and sample id never
                   transform a prediction. Static and runtime assertions. Failure
                   stops the run and is not a result.

submit = G0 AND G1 AND G2 AND G3 AND G4 AND G5 AND G6 AND G7

PRECEDENCE for verdict labels, fixed before the run so an overlap cannot be
decided after the fact:
  1  NOT(G0 and G7)                              INVALID-IMPLEMENTATION
  2  G6 at the upper grid edge on any ruler      INCONCLUSIVE
  3  G1 and G2 and G3 and G4 and G5              SUPPORTED
  4  G1 and G2 and G3 and NOT G4                 REFUTED-DIRECTION-NONSPECIFIC
  5  G1 and NOT G2                               EQUIVALENT-TO-REGULARISATION
  6  NOT G1 and G3 and G5                        BELOW-BAND
  7  NOT G5                                      UNSTABLE-k
  8  anything else                               REFUTED

Verdict meanings:
  SUPPORTED                      one production submission, recipe in section
                                 "SUBMISSION".
  BELOW-BAND                     real but under the null-adjusted bar. No sub-
                                 mission. Recorded, not dressed up.
  REFUTED                        attenuation correction does not survive honest
                                 validation. Approach A closes. No submission.
  REFUTED-DIRECTION-NONSPECIFIC  expansion helps displaced and interior soils
                                 alike: the gain is metric geometry, not atten-
                                 uation. Closes, and is the expected outcome.
  EQUIVALENT-TO-REGULARISATION   a different alpha does the same job. Approach A
                                 closes; the finding transfers to the selection
                                 question and is reported there.
  UNSTABLE-k                     the correction is not identifiable at n=24.
                                 Closes; a larger labelled corpus would be the
                                 only remedy and does not exist.
  INCONCLUSIVE                   grid truncation. NOT a refutation, and does not
                                 license a submission either. Amending the grid
                                 is a separate owner decision, never applied
                                 inside a run.
  INVALID-IMPLEMENTATION         no scientific conclusion in either direction.
                                 Fix the code, rerun, gate unchanged.


SUBMISSION RULE - EXACT, CONDITIONAL, AT MOST ONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only on SUPPORTED.
  File  Submission_Model6_A.csv, written by the notebook under that name, never
        renamed, never edited afterwards.
  Recipe  the frozen M1 E3 configuration - 12 features, rank-3, alpha selected by
        the existing nested rule on all 24 labelled soils - plus the single
        expansion step with k = k_hat, where k_hat is produced by the identical
        inner-fold parsimony rule validated above and NOT re-fitted on the test
        set. y_bar is the training mean curve of the fitting set.
  The eight structural validation gates run first and must pass.
  DECLARED DIFFERENCE: the submitted configuration is fitted on all 24 soils and
  its k_hat is estimated from training folds only; neither the 10 test soils nor
  their absent labels enter the estimate. This is stated in the record because it
  is the one place where the validated procedure and the shipped procedure could
  diverge.
  A Kaggle score is then recorded against the band below and NOTHING is changed
  in response to it. Any follow-up is a new pre-registered experiment.


EXTERNAL BAND - WRITTEN BEFORE THE SCORE EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only defined if a submission happens, since it is the score's only honest reader.
Anchor: the current best external scores are 60.56167 (M2 E1 D, a declared probe)
and 61.23560 (M1 E3). Approach A does not change what the model sees, so it
cannot plausibly buy more than the measured spread of predictions it rescales.
The N1 gains observed under ORACLE-k - the ceiling for this intervention - were
+4.2 to +11.15 EMD in-domain; nested gains were smaller and sometimes negative.
A k-expansion also degrades symmetrically if the test curves are on the other
side of the mean, which N3 says is live.

  predicted band  55.5 .. 66.0
    below 55.5  -> the correction is stronger than anything measured in-domain;
                   suspect a bug in the projection order before believing it.
    55.5 .. 60.5 -> SUPPORTED direction confirmed with useful magnitude.
    60.5 .. 61.2 -> improvement over 60.56167 is under the tie threshold; the
                    correction is real but not competition-relevant.
    61.2 .. 66.0 -> within noise of the incumbent; no claim either way.
    above 66.0   -> the displacement inference was wrong in sign. Approach A
                    closes and the null-anchored gate did its job.

The band is written now, at spec time, with no score in hand, and will be quoted
unchanged afterwards.

WHAT IS FORBIDDEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Choosing k, a threshold, an arm or a grid from Kaggle feedback.
  - A second submission from Approach A for any reason, including to check a
    hypothesis the first one raised.
  - Citing A2 (oracle-k) as the result. That is the error this spec exists to
    prevent.
  - Editing Models 1, 2 or 5, their records, contracts, thresholds or verdicts.
  - Reinterpreting the +16.98 zero-skill gap as evidence of range displacement.
    It is not; see 2.3.
  - Retrying Approach A with a per-soil k, a per-direction k or a matrix of
    gains after a refutation. That is a different hypothesis and needs its own
    pass.
