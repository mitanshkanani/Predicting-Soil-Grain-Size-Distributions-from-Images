Hypothesis: the frozen rank-3 head treats log10(D50) -> curve shape as LINEAR, and the

VERDICT:  NOT-RESOLVED on the end-to-end claim.

1. HEADLINE, THE TWO HALVES OF THIS RESULT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
They point in opposite directions and both are real.

CONFIRMED - the shape map is curved.
    Quadratic beats linear on the shape map by +6.09 EMD with the true median grain
    size held exactly fixed and NO features at all.
    Family-cluster bootstrap 95% CI [+1.278, +10.769], P(gain < 0) = 0.0073.
    Better for 19 of 24 soils. Median per-family gain +7.4 EMD.
    Degree 3 does NOT improve on degree 2 (19.36 vs 19.12), so the effect saturates at
    exactly two parameters.

NOT RESOLVED - it does not survive contact with the noisy D50 estimate.
    Wiring the same quadratic through the full feature -> D50 -> shape pipeline gains
    only +0.47 EMD against the linear control (CI [-3.876, +4.923], P = 0.433), and
    +2.66 EMD against the frozen head with the CI straddling zero.

This is the outcome section 9.9 of the plan PRE-REGISTERED as the likely one, quoted
from research_and_plan.md:

    "The label-side fix is real; the reparameterised pipeline is not yet a demonstrable
     improvement."

The experiment confirmed that prediction rather than contradicting it. That is what a
pre-registration is for.

4. THE CONFIRMED MECHANISM, IN DETAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With the true D50 supplied, the map from grain size to curve shape:

    linear    25.21 EMD
    quadratic 19.12 EMD     <- 6.09 better
    cubic     19.36 EMD     <- WORSE than quadratic

Per-family gains (quadratic minus linear, positive = quadratic better):
    FAM16 +31.1   FAM10 +17.8   FAM14 +17.2   FAM13 +16.7   FAM6  +13.0
    FAM12 +10.8   FAM11  +8.2   FAM5  +8.0    FAM4  +7.4    FAM8  +7.4
    FAM9  +3.5    FAM3  +1.9    FAM2  +1.0    FAM15 -1.0    FAM1 -6.4
    FAM7 -27.6

Worse in 3 of 16 families, dominated by one (FAM7). Median family gains 7.4 EMD, so
this is a broad effect rather than one carried by a single family.

WHY THIS MATTERS EVEN THOUGH THE END-TO-END CLAIM FAILED
The frozen head's error decomposes as roughly 25.21 of shape cost against 16.42 of D50
error (plan section 9.3). And section 9.3 also showed the shape is NOT learnable from
any feature - every feature gives NEGATIVE R-squared on the shape residual. So the
shape block could only ever be reduced by making the fixed map a better map.

This experiment says that lever exists and is worth about 6 EMD. It simply is not
reachable by predicting D50 first and then mapping, because a D50 estimate carrying
roughly 3x error in grain size is too noisy to place a soil on a curved map. That is a
statement about the ROUTE, not about the target.

The competition's co-host, Enrico Soranzo, fits a TWO-PARAMETER Weibull (GRAI3, and the

5b. AN HONEST LIMITATION ON THE NULL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The permutation null is UNDERPOWERED at n=24, and I am not going to present it as
though it were not. Over 2000 draws its gap has SD 4.82 and a p95 of +8.80 - WIDER
than the real +6.09. So the best-of-N null cannot discriminate this effect, and G4's
failure on that test is a statement about the null's resolution, not evidence against
the mechanism.

The family-cluster bootstrap CAN discriminate it (CI [+1.28, +10.77], P = 0.0073), and
that is the ruler section 9.4 pre-registered for this comparison. Where the two
instruments disagree, the pre-registered one governs and the other is reported as
inconclusive.

6. BUGS FOUND AND FIXED DURING THIS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recorded because each one would have produced a wrong answer silently.

(a) D50 interpolation convention. The natural mistake is to interpolate
    percent-versus-log-diameter instead of log-diameter-versus-percent. check_d50.py
    asserts the round-trip curve_at(Y, D50) == 50, which only the correct convention
    satisfies; the wrong one differs by up to 1.210 in log10 D50, a FACTOR OF 16 in
    diameter. It also matches the project's independently recorded logD50 column to
    2.220e-16.

(b) The first permutation null permuted curve rows, which carried each curve's own
    D50 along with it and left the relationship under test intact. It produced a
    "null" mean of +5.72 against a real +6.09 - a null that tracks the real effect.
    Corrected to hold the D50 abscissa fixed and permute only the targets.

(c) A row-indexing bug in the shape-only arm: project() returns 2-D and indexing it as
    1-D raised on shapes (22,) vs (11,).

(d) A wrong assertion in my own gate: it rejected log10 D50 < 0 as invalid, but -1.671
    is 0.021 mm, a perfectly ordinary fine soil. Only the DIAMETER must be positive.

7. WHY NO submission.csv WAS GENERATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Generating one is technically trivial here - the test features are the same 34-row CSV,
it runs locally on CPU in seconds. It is declined on evidence, not on capability:

  - The verdict is NOT-RESOLVED. The project's own rule (Model 6, Approach A) is
    SUBMIT = TRUE if and only if VERDICT == SUPPORTED.
  - The best candidate gain, +2.66 EMD, is about one tenth of what the public
    leaderboard can resolve. With per-soil SD 22.75, a 3-soil public mean has a 95%
    band near [21.7, 69.9]; the 10-soil private band is [32.6, 53.8].
  - Three of the six submissions this project has ever made were probes, not gate
    passes, including the current best of 60.56167.
  - AGENT_BRIEF.md is explicit: "Do not quote 60.56 as evidence that pretrained
    features work."

Spending a slot on a change that cannot be measured on the board, and cannot beat the
existing best by a resolvable margin, is how the project produced a 172.70.

8. WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTABLISHED (new, and reusable)
  - The log10(D50) -> shape relation is genuinely curved; two parameters suffice and a
    third does not. P = 0.0073, 19/24 soils, saturating at degree 2.
  - The end-to-end D50 reparameterisation does NOT deliver it. Predicting D50 first and
    mapping through it is the wrong route for this gain.
  - 25.21 EMD of the frozen head's error is shape, ~16.42 is D50, and the shape portion
    has a real unexploited structural correction that features cannot supply.

NOT ESTABLISHED
  - Any Kaggle improvement. None is claimed and none should be inferred.
  - Any transfer claim. Every ruler here measures interpolation on 24 labelled soils.

9. WHAT I RECOMMEND NEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shape lever is real but the D50-first route cannot pull it, because the D50
estimate is too noisy (~3x error in grain size) to place a soil on a curved map. Two
ways to reach the same 6 EMD without that noise:

  (a) Correct the shape INSIDE the existing head rather than replacing it. Keep the
      rank-3 basis and the ridge, and let the residual be modelled with a quadratic
      term in log10 D50 that is estimated jointly rather than in a second stage. This
      is the more promising route because it never requires a hard D50 placement.

  (b) Attack the colour extrapolation (plan section 9.5), which is the only measured,
      mechanistically explained lead for the 43 -> 61 internal-to-external gap: 0 of 10
      test soils have a close training neighbour, and ALL of the extrapolation is in
      the colour block, with B exceeding the training range by 3.33 SD on 9 of 10 soils.

My recommendation is (a) first, as Experiment 2. It is the direct follow-up to a
mechanism this experiment just confirmed, it needs no new features and no GPU, and it
targets a block that no feature engineering has ever been able to touch.
