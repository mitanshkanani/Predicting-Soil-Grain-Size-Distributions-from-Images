3. WHAT THIS RULES OUT, AND WHY IT MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The camera finding was REAL and is not in question: 0 iPhone images in training, 35 in test,
grey-world gains differing systematically by camera, blue 3.33 SD beyond training range.

But being real is not the same as being the binding constraint. Experiment 3 shows the camera
shift is something the model is already robust TO. The handcrafted head absorbs a complete
camera change and delivers the same score regardless of how colour is encoded. That is
evidence the frozen head is genuinely camera-invariant already - which is exactly why it
survived the camera split when DINOv2 lost by 4.76 EMD.

So the 43 -> 56 story is now:

    internal CV measures interpolation on 24 Motorola/Samsung soils   ~43
    the model transfers to 10 iPhone soils at all                      yes, 55.8
    the gap is caused by feature-set choice                          CONFIRMED (E2, 11.46 EMD)
    the gap is caused by colour encoding                             REFUTED (E3, 1.79 EMD)
    the gap is caused by regularisation                              REFUTED (E2 alpha, optimal at 3)

4. THE INTERNAL RULER - A CORRECTION I OWE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Experiment 2 arms: internal rho = -1.000   (perfect anti-rank)
    Experiment 3 arms: internal rho = +0.500   (positive, n = 3)

I wrote in plan.md section 4, as a PREDICTION before these scores existed: "If the same thing
happens here, the arm our ruler likes MOST will lose."

That prediction was WRONG. E3-A had the best internal score (40.79) and the best external score
of the three (56.09). The anti-rank did not repeat.

This is a correction to something I asserted confidently, so it gets stated plainly: the
anti-ranking is NOT a stable law. It was one observed ordering at n = 3. E3 is a second sample
and it came out +0.500. Any claim that "internal CV anti-ranks arms" should be read as "in E2
it did, once, and we do not yet know how often."

That weakens the tidy narrative I gave you earlier and it is worth having weakened, because a
n = 3 ordering from a single experiment was never strong enough to build on.

5. THE REAL FINDING, AND IT IS NOT ABOUT COLOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Across five external submissions of the SAME head at the SAME alpha, varying only the colour
treatment:

    E2-A  raw RGB                55.80591   <- project best
    E3-A  CIELAB                56.08671
    E3-C  +2 texture columns    56.43951
    E2-a0.3                      59.38746
    E3-B  grey-world corrected   57.87360
    E2-C  rank-normalised        62.51669
    E2-B  colour deleted         67.26598

The best and worst differ by 11.46 EMD, and the worst is the one with colour REMOVED. Colour is
worth about 11 EMD and the model cannot work without it. But the ENCODING of that colour is
worth under 2 EMD.

That is the finding: this model extracts what it needs from colour and is indifferent to how
colour is written down. Any further gain has to come from a different direction - more
information, not a better parameterisation of what we already have.

transfer here, and the model is already insensitive to colour encoding.

7. WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, AND WHAT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESTABLISHED
  - H3 is refuted. Four distinct colour encodings span 1.79 EMD on the public board.
  - The camera split is real and mechanistically documented, but it is NOT the binding
    constraint - the model already absorbs it.
  - Colour is worth 11.46 EMD (deleting it costs that much) and its ENCODING is worth under 2.
  - The internal ruler did NOT anti-rank these arms (rho = +0.500), correcting my E2-based claim.
  - Both registered anchors reproduced bit-exactly; the CIELAB transform is exact to 1e-9.

NOT ESTABLISHED
  - Whether E3-A is truly equal to E2-A. A 0.28 EMD gap at 0.02 SD is not a difference.
  - Whether grey-world gains carry soil information. E3-B's 2.07 EMD loss is 0.16 SD.
  - Whether ANY of these differences would survive the private board, which carries 70%.
  - Whether a fundamentally different model class would do better. This experiment varied the
    feature ENCODING only. It cannot speak to CNNs, transformers, or learned embeddings.

8. WHERE THE PROJECT STANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ten submissions. Best 55.80591, unchanged since Experiment 2. The last six submissions have all
landed between 55.8 and 62.5, and the four best are within 0.7 EMD of each other.

That is the plateau, stated plainly: we are not moving. Three experiments have now tested
feature set (E2, 11.46 EMD of movement), regularisation (E2, optimal), and colour encoding
(E3, 1.79 EMD). Two of those three were the available levers and neither moved us.

The honest conclusion is the one pre-registered in plan.md section 6: further tuning of THIS
head is not merely slow, it is uninformative. Twelve handcrafted features and 24 soils appear to
be the binding constraint.

9. WHAT I WOULD DO NEXT, AND THE HONEST DIFFICULTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the intent is to move the score, the next step has to be MORE INFORMATION, because that is
the only dimension the evidence supports. Options, none of them cheap:

  (a) Per-image features instead of per-soil medians. The pipeline currently collapses each
      soil to ONE row by taking the median over qualifying tiles. The 127 train JPGs carry
      information that the median throws away. This is the largest untapped source in the
      project and it needs no new sensor data.
  (b) A model class that can use the images directly. But note honestly: DINOv2 LOST to the
      handcrafted head by 4.76 EMD here, so "just use a big pretrained model" is not supported
      by anything we have measured.
  (c) Accept 55.80591 and spend remaining submissions on something orthogonal.

What I would NOT do is another round of feature-block swaps on these 12 columns. Experiment 3
just measured that this model is insensitive to how those columns are encoded, and
re-encoding produced 1.79 EMD of movement inside the noise band.
