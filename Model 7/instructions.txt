THE M5 CLOSURE BOUNDARY, RESOLVED AND RECORDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Question asked: does Model 5's permanent closure cover ALL use of unlabeled
  test-image statistics?

  Answer: NO. All three records scope the closure to a mechanism, not to a data
  source.

    - AGENT_BRIEF.md section 12 closes "label-free camera adaptation (CORAL /
      moment matching, incl. transductive use of the unlabeled test images)".
    - Model 5 spec section 10 schedules no "follow-up alignment variant".
    - Model 5/instructions.txt makes a re-run with a different alignment target
      "a NEW pre-registered experiment requiring an explicit owner decision".

  The distinction: Model 5 estimated a MAP taking the source feature distribution
  onto the target's, by matching means and covariances, with camera-defined
  directions, and re-mapped the inputs. M7-T proposed a fixed-rank subspace and
  penalty geometry with no distributional matching and no camera identity. That
  is outside the letter of the closure.

  What cuts the other way: the same section 12 block states that the in-domain
  headroom is not reachable "nor by adapting the features to the test
  distribution (Model 5 tested that and closed)", and M7-T's motivation was
  exactly to make the estimator fit the deployment distribution's geometry.

  Disposition: M7-T is suspended, not preregistered. The ambiguity is not read as
  permission. It returns only on an explicit owner ruling that the sentence meant
  distribution matching specifically, and then only as a new pre-registered
  experiment whose first clause is an inertness check - at 12 dimensions and rank
  3 the train+test pooled subspace may be nearly identical to the train-only one,
  which would make the arm structurally inert rather than merely null. The prize
  is small in any case: the camera term measured by Model 5 is about 5.55 EMD.

  Self-correction kept on the record: the first draft of this ruling answered
  "yes, the closure covers it" and killed M7-T on the parenthetical alone. That
  over-read all three records.
