    43.0217308796477 and the G1 >= 3.00 EMD threshold UNCHANGED.

  - No tuning, no feature expansion, no post-hoc selection after seeing the CV

THE FROZEN BLOCK - these are the experiment's variables. Nothing else may vary.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Inputs added   : exactly four statistics, ids A1-A4, as defined in the spec.
                   K = 4. No fifth statistic. No scale sweep. No free parameter
                   per statistic. Lags fixed from the tile geometry before the
                   run (a 256 tile spans 56.23263875 mm at 4.5525 px/mm).
  Everything else: the Model 1 E3 / Model 2 E3 pipeline, byte-for-byte -
                   the 12 hand features, the tile->image median, the
                   image->soil median, StandardScaler, the rank-3 curve basis,
                   ridge with the frozen nested alpha rule, the monotone
                   projection, the trapezoid EMD.
  Fold unit      : CV family (16 families over 24 soils). A scored soil's own
                   family is never in its fitting set.
  Resampling     : family-clustered paired bootstrap, 4000 draws.
  Anchor (G0)    : the pipeline WITHOUT the new block reproduces
                   43.0217308796477 with max deviation 0.00e+00, computed before
                   any arm is scored.
  Success bar    : G1 gain >= 3.00 EMD internal CV mean reduction.

WHAT IS FORBIDDEN, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Choosing K, choosing among statistics, or changing a lag after seeing any
    CV number. The measured best-of-N selection null is 15.29 EMD median for
    15 heads; a best-of-4 null is measured for this experiment instead of
    inherited.
  - Using the public leaderboard (baseline 60.56167, Model 2 E1) to pick a
    model, a subset or a threshold. Internal CV EMD and external Kaggle EMD are
    different scales and are never compared arithmetically.
  - Camera, device, ppm, EXIF, ICC profile, site or sample id as a feature, a
    selection key or a dispatch key.
  - Writing to data/Training, data/Test or data/processed_meta, or re-running
    preprocessing. Tiles are READ.
  - Any random image-level split.
  - Reporting MEAN-style exact zeros as a measured null; they are structural.
  - Reading CAM+RES as anything but an ordinal instrument; it may not rank arms
    separated by less than the 15 EMD ordinal floor and may never select.
  - Overwriting or renaming any previous experiment's results or notebooks.
  - Re-cutting the alias pair list after seeing the gate outcome. The pair set
    is generated by the deterministic rule in the spec and must first reproduce
    the Model 6-3 figures that rule owns (top-10 share 61.2%, worst-soil 10.1%).
    Amendment A1 (2026-09-30): the median alias curve gap of 65.7 EMD is NOT a
    reproduction target here - it came from Model 6-3's own worst-8/best-8
    unconstrained neighbour rule, a different instrument, and the registered
    rule measures 56.0966 by construction. Both directions stay asserted.
  - Reviving the withdrawn leaky 1-NN bound or the withdrawn 1-1.5 EMD cap.
  - Treating the M5 closure ambiguity as permission for M7-T.

ORDER OF OPERATIONS - the run may not skip ahead
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0. Prerequisite: G0 anchor reproduced to 0.00e+00.
  1. Alias-separability pre-gate on the fixed block. If it fails, M7-A closes
     here with no CV arm scored, and M7-C becomes the next experiment.
  2. Inertness check G6: zeroing the new columns must reproduce the anchor
     exactly, proving the plumbing can move the metric.
  3. Controls that must be able to differ: P-RAND (phase-scrambled), P-SHUF
     (per-column shuffled), P-COLS (linear combinations of the existing 12).
  4. Best-of-4 permutation null P-SEL, measured before the gated result is used.
  5. The gated arm, then the family-clustered paired bootstrap.
  6. Verdict by the interpretation table only. Submission only on SUPPORTED.

GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  G0 anchor     : no-block pipeline reproduces 43.0217308796477, deviation 0.0
  G1 effect     : mean gain >= 3.00 EMD internal CV
  G2 support    : family-clustered paired bootstrap CI excludes 0
  G3 specificity: gain exceeds BOTH P-RAND and P-SHUF by more than the paired
                  noise partner
  G4 null       : gain exceeds the measured best-of-4 permutation null
  G5 validity   : determinism across two runs; all cells finite; NaN rate
                  reported and identical between arm and control
  G6 inertness  : new columns zeroed reproduces the anchor exactly
  G7 honesty    : no test-split statistic, camera label, ppm or sample id in
                  any feature, selection or fold assignment

SUBMISSION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SUBMIT = TRUE if and only if VERDICT == SUPPORTED. Every other verdict means
  no submission, including SUPPORTED-BELOW-BAND. The submission CSV is generated
  by the notebook under its final name, against the existing submission schema,
  and is never renamed. The external band is written down before any score
  exists. One submission maximum. If M7-A is rejected by its pre-gates or fails
  G1, the next registered step is evaluating M7-C as the cheap remaining
  existing-pipeline experiment - not another transform on M7-A's data.
