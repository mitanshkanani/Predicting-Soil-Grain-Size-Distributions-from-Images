MODEL 2 - EXPERIMENT 3 - DOES THE EMBEDDING ADD INFORMATION, OR ONLY COLUMNS?
================================================================================

HYPOTHESIS UNDER TEST - H3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concatenating the 12 hand-built features with the 384-dimensional frozen DINOv2 tile
embedding yields a LOWER nested in-domain EMD than the 12 hand features alone, by more than
the same concatenation with a label-blind row-permuted embedding block, at Model 2 E1's
frozen magnification.

We do not assume H3 is true. P3 below predicts it is REFUTED. E3 exists to find out which,
with controls that make a positive result attributable to information rather than to
capacity.


WHAT E1 AND E2 ACTUALLY ESTABLISHED - READ THIS BEFORE INTERPRETING E3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1 tested REPLACEMENT: do 384 DINOv2 features beat 12 hand features? REFUTED. In-domain
LOGO-CV 44.33 against 43.02. Its durable finding is that D is about 5x more camera-stable
(camera/soil 0.209 against M1's 1.105) and about 28x more blur-stable (blur/soil 0.029
against 0.818) while being LESS accurate: robustness and informativeness are independent.
E1 also recorded PATCH_MM = 3.0752 mm, which omits the 256 -> 224 resize.

E2 tested RESCUE BY SCALE: does E1's refutation survive correcting that number and sweeping
patch magnification eightfold (crop 256/128/64/32, patch 3.5145 down to 0.4393 mm)?
REFUTED. D lost to its own random-init twin R at every one of the four crops, with no trend
(rho = -0.40, p = 0.60) and no gain located in the patch-limited fines. No submission was
produced; the gate failed all its clauses.

E2's MACHINERY passed its own regression checks (crop 256 reproduced E1's M1 mean to 0.0017
EMD; the control matrix matched Model 1 E3's to 2.8e-14). What is refuted is the
HYPOTHESIS. E3 inherits the machinery and the frozen configuration. E3 inherits NO belief
that magnification helped, and crop 128 is not carried forward as a preference despite being
D's best crop in E2.

THE QUESTION NEITHER ASKED: do D's columns carry information the hand features lack? That
is complementarity, and it is the only reading left under which a frozen backbone matters
to this target at all.


THE ONE VARIABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The feature matrix composition: 12 columns become 12 + 384 = 396.

FROZEN AND UNTOUCHED: every preprocessing artifact and everything under data/; tile size
256 px; the soil-fraction filter at 0.50; the tile -> image -> soil median aggregation; the
StandardScaler treatment exactly as E1/E2 applied it; the rank-3 curve basis; closed-form
ridge; the monotone projection (clip -> cummax -> force 100 at 200 mm); the 16 CV families
over the same 24 soils; the alpha grid 0.03 ... 1e6; the E4 parsimony rule (largest alpha
within 15.0 EMD of the grid optimum, final fit only); trapezoid EMD; n_boot 4000; n_perm
200; config_hash 010f44c36c74; embed_seed 20260930 (the harvested cache filenames contain
it - changing it does not merely re-key the cache, it silently invalidates E3 entirely).

NOT EDITED, BY RULE: backbones.py, check_backbones.py, crop_geometry.py,
check_crop_geometry.py. E3 does not import torch or timm at all, and imports
crop_geometry only for the printed geometry table.

DELIBERATELY OUT OF SCOPE: a curve-space blend head, PCA of the 384, feature selection
inside the block, per-block alpha, fine-tuning, any other backbone, re-tiling,
label-adaptive anything, and camera / ppm / EXIF / site / sample id as model inputs.


ARMS - NINE, AND ONLY ONE CARRIES THE GATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1  M1          12       the external anchor; must reproduce 43.0217
  2  D@256       384      the embedding alone; must reproduce 44.3302 - this is also the
                          proof the harvested arrays really are E2's
  3  R@256       384      the random-init twin alone; must reproduce 39.7769. Required by
                          Model 2's standing capacity-control rule (instructions line 98):
                          M1, R and D must all be present or the gate may not evaluate
  4  FUSE@256    396      THE GATED ARM - H3 itself
  5  SHUF@256    396      12 + the D block with its soil-to-embedding pairing destroyed by a
                          seed-pinned ROW permutation. Same column count, same per-column
                          scaling, same covariance; no correspondence to the soil. This is
                          what separates "more columns" from "more information"
  6  FR@256      396      12 + the R block. Is any gain pretraining-specific, or would any
                          ViT-shaped block do?
  7  FUSE@128    396      sensitivity only, cannot fire the gate
  8  FUSE@64     396      sensitivity only, cannot fire the gate
  9  FUSE@32     396      sensitivity only, cannot fire the gate

The permutation is applied to the 24 soil rows of the embedding table, never to the hand
block, never informed by the targets, generated from permutation_seed 20260933, and its
vector is written to cv_per_soil.csv so the control is reconstructable by hand.


THE DECISION GATE - FIXED BEFORE THE RUN, EXECUTED AS CODE IN CELL O1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    G1  in_domain(FUSE@256) <  in_domain(M1)      AND paired soil-bootstrap CI of
                                                  (M1 - FUSE) excludes zero, FUSE better
    G2  in_domain(FUSE@256) <  in_domain(SHUF@256) AND paired CI of (SHUF - FUSE)
                                                  excludes zero
    G3  in_domain(FUSE@256) <  in_domain(D@256)   [dominance; no CI required]

    PROVISO  0.03 < oracle_alpha(FUSE@256) < 1e6   else verdict = INCONCLUSIVE
    VALID    in_domain(SHUF@256) >  in_domain(M1)   else verdict = INSTRUMENT INVALID
    COMPLETE {M1, D@256, R@256} all present         else evaluable = False

    submit = G1 AND G2 AND G3 AND PROVISO AND VALID AND COMPLETE

Thresholds, set before any number existed: comparators 43.02173087964769 (M1),
44.330245615450906 (D@256), 39.77693008453303 (R@256); reproduction tolerance 0.05 EMD per
control arm; bootstrap 4000 soil resamples, percentile CI, significance = interval excludes
0.0; practically-meaningful band 15.0 EMD (E4's ORDINAL_FLOOR).

G3 is implied by G1 given E2's measured values and is kept explicit anyway, because the
claim under test is that fusion beats EITHER family alone, and because the reproduction in
H3 could move D@256.

Verdict is one of: SUBMIT, REFUTED, CAPACITY-NOT-INFORMATION, INCONCLUSIVE,
INSTRUMENT INVALID.


WHEN A SUBMISSION MAY BE WRITTEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only if submit is True. Then the arm fitted is FUSE@256 - always FUSE@256, never the best of
the four fused crops - and the file is written by the notebook itself as
Submission_Model2_E3.csv, after the eight validation gates, and is never renamed afterwards.

If any clause fails, NOTHING IS WRITTEN and the notebook says why. That is the expected
outcome (P3) and it is a complete answer, not an unfinished run.

THERE IS NO PROBE IN THIS EXPERIMENT. probe_arm = None. E1's probe was a separate decision
made after its gate failed, explicitly approved, and is recorded as a probe rather than a
gate pass. E3 does not pre-authorise a repeat of that.


PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  P1  [not blind] M1, D@256 and R@256 reproduce 43.0217, 44.3302 and 39.7769 to within
      0.05 EMD each. Plumbing and provenance; failure aborts the notebook.
  P2  [BLIND] SHUF@256 is WORSE than M1. Three hundred and eighty-four standardised noise
      columns on 24 soils should cost accuracy. If SHUF beats M1, the CV is rewarding
      capacity and nothing else in this report is read.
  P3  [BLIND, THE MAIN ONE] FUSE@256 does NOT beat M1 with a CI excluding zero. H3 is
      refuted. Rationale stated in advance: E1 and E2 both found D no more informative than
      its own random-init twin, which is not the signature of a block carrying extra
      information. A REFUTED P3 is the interesting result.
  P4  [BLIND] If FUSE does beat M1, the gain is under about 2 EMD - an order of magnitude
      below the 15.0 band. The D-versus-M1 gap measured in E1/E2 was 0.3 to 1.9 EMD.
  P5  [BLIND] FUSE's camera/soil ratio lands between D's 0.209 and M1's 1.105. Mechanism,
      reported only, never gating.
  P6  [BLIND] FR@256 behaves like FUSE@256, so any gain is "more ViT-shaped columns" rather
      than pretraining.
  P7  [BLIND] No fused sensitivity crop (128/64/32) beats the fused gate crop.

HOW E3 RUNS - AND WHY THAT IS DIFFERENT FROM E1 AND E2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Local CPU only. E3 imports no torch, needs no GPU and needs no Kaggle run. Decided with the
user on 2026-09-29: no dataset upload, no zip, and the existing
final_kaggle_upload_m2e2.zip is untouched.

E3 reads the tile embeddings E2 already produced. Required inputs, 30 files, 79.6 MB:
  embeddings/embed_<backend>_c<crop>_256_010f44c36c74_20260930<view>.npy  (+ each
  .paths.npy sibling) for dinov2 at crops 256/128/64/32 and vit_random at crop 256, each in
  views real, _res14, _res16. Real-view arrays are (1976, 384) float64, res arrays
  (1541, 384).

E3 HAS NO MOCK MODE. This is a deliberate structural change from E1 and E2. Their
BACKEND=mock path is the mechanism that let E1's ndarray.median crash survive to Kaggle and
that emptied whole sections during E2's dry run. Here the only input is the real arrays; a
missing file raises and lists every one that is absent. There is no branch that runs locally
and a different branch remotely, so there is no untested branch.

Consequence, stated plainly: for E3 the local run IS the real experiment. The answer to H3
is known before anything is submitted anywhere.
