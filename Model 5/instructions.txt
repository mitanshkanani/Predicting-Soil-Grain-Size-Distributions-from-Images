MODEL 5 - LABEL-FREE DOMAIN ADAPTATION FOR THE CAMERA SHIFT
================================================================================

STATUS: CLOSED AT ONE EXPERIMENT, 2026-09-29. Verdict DIRECTION-INCONSISTENT, H5 REFUTED,
        submit=False, no submission produced. Nothing here may be reopened without an explicit
        owner decision.


WHAT THIS MODEL REPRESENTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model 5 asked one question that no earlier experiment had made its primary variable: can the
camera shift be removed WITHOUT labels, by mapping the fitting camera's feature distribution onto
the unlabeled test images and then predicting?

Models 1 and 2 had both closed having never treated the shift itself as the target. Model 5 attacked
it with closed-form CORAL alignment at image level - the cheapest, most assumption-free member of
the family - and gated the claim on SPECIFICITY rather than on a production analogue: fit camera A,
align toward the iPhone test images, score camera B, a camera that was neither fitted on nor the
alignment target. That is the one construction that cannot be accused of fitting the answer.


WHAT WAS ESTABLISHED, AND THE MEASUREMENT THAT PICKED THIS MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The model was chosen by measuring each remaining loss rather than by taste:

  output parameterisation (rank-3 -> rank-5 basis)   ~4.9 EMD total available   -> Model 3 dropped
  prediction-level curve fusion                      ~0.9 EMD, unregistered
                                                     spike, indistinguishable   -> closed
  camera transfer                                    the largest, untouched      -> Model 5

The camera figure used to justify that ordering was +16.2 / +18.2 EMD. See the correction below:
that number was a composite, and the camera-only component is about a third of it.


RESULT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H5 REFUTED. Aligning toward the unlabeled test images made cross-camera transfer WORSE on the
pooled 45 rows and inconsistent across the two measurable directions.

  gain(CORAL) over the unaligned control, soil-clustered 95% CI (positive = better):
      pooled  -8.748  [-12.776, -3.967]
      A       -18.464  [-24.843, -12.187]   Motorola fitted -> Samsung scored: a collapse
      B        +0.546  [ -6.482,  +8.222]   Samsung fitted -> Motorola scored: flat

  gate: G0 PASS (control reproduced E3's 43.0217308796477 exactly)  G1 FAIL  G2 FAIL  G3 FAIL
        G4 FAIL  G5 PASS  G6 PASS (no direction alpha-clipped)  G7 PASS
        -> DIRECTION-INCONSISTENT, and treated as refuted for decisions per spec section 6.
  closure: gain(CORAL) missed G1 with NO boundary caveat, which is exactly section 10's stated
        condition, so MODEL 5 CLOSES AT ONE EXPERIMENT.

  The other arms, pooled gain: MEAN -0.000, CORAL_SELF -0.000, CORAL_TILE -0.391,
  CORAL_SOIL -7.784, CORAL_INDEP -13.446.

WHAT SURVIVES FROM THIS, WHICH IS NOT THE HYPOTHESIS
  1. The specificity instrument WORKED. The same-marginal placebo (CORAL_INDEP, which preserves
     every per-feature mean, variance and marginal of the real target and destroys only the joint
     structure) degraded even further than CORAL did in the failing direction, -27.582 against
     -18.464. The two arms separate, in the direction the design was built to detect. A null from a
     ruler that cannot distinguish its own arms would have been worthless; this one is not.
  2. MEAN and CORAL_SELF turned out to be STRUCTURAL zeros, not measured ones. The MEAN arm is a
     constant shift; the soil median commutes with it; the frozen StandardScaler deletes it. So
     MEAN is identical to NONE to 6.9e-15, and any pre-registered reading of "the mean shift
     carries most of it" (P3) is the identity talking. In a pipeline whose head standardises its
     inputs, a first-moment-only adaptation CANNOT do anything. That is a transferable fact about
     this pipeline, and any future adaptation must therefore be evaluated on its second-moment
     content or on features the head does not rescale.
  3. Alpha-boundary reporting earned its place: every nested alpha was INTERIOR (1 to 3), and the
     oracle sat at the grid ceiling in only 1 of 16 folds, so the failure cannot be blamed on the
     grid. But the eval-side grid spread averaged 56 to 101 EMD per fold, which says cross-camera
     error in this design is dominated by regularisation strength, and the fit-side nested rule
     systematically under-regularises for it.
  4. Transductive use of the 35 unlabeled test images was allowed by the owner and is now a tested
     idea with a negative result. It did not need labels and did not help.


THE CORRECTED CAMERA-PENALTY DECOMPOSITION - IMPORTANT FOR ANY FUTURE DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model 5's own measurement (cross-camera mean error minus the same-camera in-domain control, inside
the training distribution, 45 rows) is:

      base mean A 51.784, B 45.366; in-domain control 43.022   ->  PENALTY = 5.55 EMD

The +18.2 figure that justified prioritising this model is NOT the same quantity. It is Model 1 E3's
nested in-domain 43.02 against its EXTERNAL leaderboard 61.24, a gap that bundles at least three
different things:

      in-domain CV -> external LB gap            +18.22   (composite, previously labelled "camera")
        of which camera transfer, measured here   ~ 5.6
        remainder, not yet decomposed             ~12.7   unseen-soil generalisation, plus the
                                                          fixed 3-of-10 public subset, plus any
                                                          residual preprocessing/photographic
                                                          difference between the sets

The prior attribution therefore overstated the camera's share by roughly a factor of three, and
Model 5 spent its budget on about a third of what it believed it was attacking. Anyone choosing a
next model from "the camera costs 18 EMD" is working from a number this project has now measured
against. The ~12.7 EMD remainder is the larger unlabelled loss and has never been decomposed.

CORRECTION, added 2026-09-29 after the M6 candidate review - the text above is left verbatim as
history. The final sentence and the "remainder ~12.7" line are WITHDRAWN.

  What is wrong: treating a 24-soil in-domain mean minus a 3-soil public mean as a measured
  quantity. The per-soil honest CV errors of the shipped model have mean 43.02 and SD 22.75, so a
  3-soil mean drawn from that distribution has null SD 12.28 and a 95% band of [21.7, 69.9]. Both
  observed public scores - M1 E3 at 61.24 and M2 E1 D at 60.56 - fall INSIDE that band. The
  zero-skill probe fails the same test: the constant train-mean curve scores 85.39 in-domain, its
  3-soil null band is [56.6, 110.2], and the observed 102.37 carries p = 0.09. So the "population
  shift" of +16.98 and the "unexplained ~12.7" are not measurements of the test population; they
  are consistent with the sampling noise of averaging three soils.

  What still stands, unchanged: the camera term is genuinely measured at +5.55 EMD, inside the
  training distribution on 45 fold-honest rows, and the model-specific external residual is 0 to
  +3.1 EMD. Those came from labelled data with n=24 and n=45, not from a 3-point mean.

  Consequence for any future model here: differences below about 25 EMD cannot be measured on the
  public set at all. Decisions must be made on the honest 24-soil CV mean with a confidence
  interval, and a submission should be expected to be unverifiable publicly until the private
  10-soil result arrives.
