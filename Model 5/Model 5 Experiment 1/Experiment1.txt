16. DISCLOSURE - TWO IMPLEMENTATION DEFECTS CORRECTED DURING THIS RUN
   A first attempt at this run aborted in cell F1 on its own n_clusters assertion, before the
   gate was evaluated and before any score was written. The assertion was right and the code
   was wrong. Two defects were found, both in the difference-and-bootstrap helpers (F1, F2),
   both corrected to match the contract as ALREADY WRITTEN in instructions.txt and spec
   section 3 - no threshold, arm, seed, precedence, clause or interpretation rule changed:
     (1) SIGN. Delta was computed as err_arm - err_NONE. The contract defines
         Delta(s) = err_NONE(s) - err_arm(s), positive meaning the arm is better. The inverted
         form would have reported an 8.75 EMD DEGRADATION as an 8.75 EMD IMPROVEMENT, and had
         G2 been satisfied it would have produced SUPPORTED and written a submission file on a
         result that harms the model.
     (2) CLUSTER UNIT. Pooled bootstrap keys were prefixed with the direction ('A:S07'), so 45
         rows became 45 clusters and the soil-clustered bootstrap silently degenerated to the
         row bootstrap it was specified to replace. Keys are now the bare soil identity: 45
         rows, 24 clusters, 21 of them spanning both directions.
   WHAT WAS OBSERVED BEFORE THE FIX, and it is reported here rather than hidden: the per-arm
   means and per-fold alphas were already on screen and are UNAFFECTED by either defect (they
   come from cells upstream of F1). Direction A arm means 51.784 / 70.248 / 79.366 for
   NONE / CORAL / CORAL_INDEP, direction B 45.366 / 44.820 / 45.291, are the same numbers this
   record prints in section 5. Only the CIs and the sign of every Delta changed.
   MEASURED EFFECT OF DEFECT (2), reported because it contradicts a prediction in the contract:
   for THIS data the soil-clustered intervals are NARROWER than the row-level ones, not wider
   (CORAL: soil [-12.78,-3.97] width 8.81 vs row [-14.34,-3.10] width 11.23; CORAL_SOIL 8.35
   vs 11.41). The sqrt(2)-narrowing argument is about within-cluster correlation and its
   DIRECTION depends on the sign of that correlation, which the contract assumed rather than
   measured. Row resampling is still invalid here - two rows for one soil are not two
   observations - so the correction stands, but the contract's stated expectation about which
   way it moves the interval was wrong and is corrected as a fact, not argued away.
   Neither defect changes the verdict: with the sign fixed and the old clustering the verdict
   is still DIRECTION-INCONSISTENT, and with the old sign the same label is reached by
   accident. The sign fix is what turns G3 from a spurious pass into a fail.
   A regression test now pins both properties by executing the shipped F1 helpers against a
   synthetic mirror of the design: scratch/check_m5_delta.py. It failed 8 ways on the pre-fix
   code and passes 14 on this one, and cell F1 recomputes one soil's Delta by hand and asserts
   the cluster count at run time so the notebook can no longer produce an inverted or
   row-level number silently. G0 was 43.0217308796477 (gap 0.00e+00) in both attempts, and the
   emitted CSVs are byte-identical across independent runs of the corrected notebook.

17. WHAT THE CAMERA PENALTY ACTUALLY MEASURED, AND A PREMISE CORRECTED
   The penalty defined and measured here (cross-camera mean minus same-camera in-domain
   control) is 5.55 EMD, not the +18.2 quoted in Model 2 E1's record. The two are not
   the same quantity: +18.2 is M1's nested in-domain 43.02 against its EXTERNAL leaderboard
   61.24, a gap that bundles unseen-soil generalisation, camera transfer and the fixed 3-of-10
   public subset. The camera-only component, measured inside the training distribution on 45
   rows, is roughly a third of it. Model 5 therefore attacked a target whose size had been
   overstated by conflating two gaps, and P5's 'no arm closes more than 25%' was evaluated
   against 5.55. Any future decision about where the remaining loss lives should
   start from this decomposition rather than from 18.2.

18. TWO READINGS THAT MUST NOT BE OVER-INTERPRETED
   P3 came back CONFIRMED and X1 'covariance adds nothing measurable', but both are artifacts
   of an identity, not evidence: the MEAN arm is a constant shift, the soil median commutes
   with it, and the frozen StandardScaler then removes it, so MEAN is EXACTLY NONE by
   construction (proved in scratch/verify_m5_result.py: max scaled-feature difference 6.9e-15).
   CORAL_SELF is likewise the identity to 3.7e-15, so G5 is a real canary that can only fire on
   a wiring fault. X1's label is worded for the case where MEAN carries a gain; here MEAN
   carries nothing at all, and the correct reading is that the covariance half COST 8.75 EMD.
   The honest positive result is that the instrument worked: the placebo degraded even more
   than CORAL did (-27.58 vs -18.46 in direction A), so the two arms separate in the
   direction the design was built to detect.
