H5 - THE HYPOTHESIS UNDER TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aligning the fitting camera's image-level feature distribution onto the unlabeled iPhone
test-image distribution, by a closed-form CORAL transform, reduces mean EMD on a camera that was
neither fitted on nor used as the alignment target, relative to the identical unaligned pipeline.

H5 IS NOT ASSUMED TRUE. P1 predicts it is REFUTED. Stated in advance: Model 2 showed the
informative structure of these images is not expressible as a linear re-mapping of these features;
the camera shift includes resolution and texture changes a feature-space affine transform cannot
undo once features are aggregated to image medians; and Model 1's E7 found cheap invariance fixes
were either circular or dependent on device identity.

A pass would mean the mechanism is real on the two measurable camera pairs. It would NOT forecast a
Kaggle score: the production target is iPhone and iPhone is never scored locally, CAM+RES is
ordinal-only by Model 1 E4's ruling, and the public leaderboard is a fixed 3 of 10 soils while the
final ranking is 0.30*public + 0.70*private.

THE ONE VARIABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A transform inserted between image-median aggregation and soil aggregation:

    tiles -> image median -> ALIGN -> soil median -> frozen head

The transform acts on the 12 hand features at IMAGE level and is applied identically to the fitting
camera and the scoring camera, so a model trained in target space is scored in target space. The
baseline arm is the identity applied to both.

FROZEN AND UNTOUCHED: everything under data/; the 12 control features
  (e4, e8, e16, lum_sd, grad_mean, R, G, B, sat, lum_p10, lum_p50, lum_p90); tile->image->soil
  median aggregation; StandardScaler; rank-3 curve basis; closed-form ridge; the alpha grid
  0.03 ... 1000000.0; monotone projection (clip -> cummax -> force 100 at 200 mm); trapezoid EMD on
  log10 d over the 11 ISO supports; the 16 curve-distance CV families; nested alpha selection; the
  Model 1 E4 parsimony rule; config_hash 010f44c36c74.

PROHIBITED, BY RULE AND BY ASSERTION: camera name or family, device make or model, ppm, EXIF, ICC
  profile, site and sample id - never as features, conditioning variables or lookup keys the
  transform can dispatch on. Test labels do not exist and are never referenced. The alignment
  function's only arguments are feature matrices; a static test and a runtime test both assert it.

FOLD-LEVEL LEAKAGE RULE (spec section 14): no soil is ever scored by a model that was fitted
on it. Each direction is split into CV folds; a fold drops one family from the fitting camera,
estimates alignment from the surviving fitting-camera images plus the 35 test images, and
scores that family's evaluation-camera images. The contracted 22 + 23 = 45 rows and 24 clusters
are unchanged; what changes is that all 45 rows come from models that never saw their soil.

THE ALIGNMENT TARGET IS THE 35 iPHONE TEST IMAGES ONLY. A runtime assertion requires the target
  image set disjoint from every training-camera image. That disjointness is what keeps the
  evaluation non-circular by construction.

                  feature mean, variance and marginal distri-  correction exactly and differs only
                  bution of the real test set preserved,      in whether the matched covariance
                  cross-feature covariance destroyed           structure is the real one
  5  CORAL_SELF    CORAL toward the fitting camera's own       implementation canary; mathemati-

variance rescaling, i.e. regularisation.

THE ROW-PERMUTATION PLACEBO THAT WAS REPLACED (spec section 13, kept so it cannot return)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The first approved placebo permuted the target's ROWS. That is provably inert: CORAL reads the
target only through its mean vector and covariance matrix, and both are invariant to row order -
measured on a correlated 12-column table, permuting rows changed the mean by 1.1e-16 and the
covariance by 4.4e-16. The difference CORAL - CORAL_PERM would have been exactly 0.00 in every run,
G4 could never have passed, and the verdict would have read REGULARISATION-ARTEFACT regardless of
the data. E3's identical-looking control was valid there because its shuffled block fed a
predictive model, where the row-to-label pairing is the signal being destroyed; an adaptation target
supplies only moments, and moments have no pairing to destroy. check_alignment.py pins the
row-permutation invariance as a test so this cannot be reintroduced.


THE DECISION GATE - FIXED BEFORE THE RUN, EXECUTED AS CODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  G0  SANITY        the full 24-soil, unaligned, no-holdout configuration reproduces Model 2 E3's
                    recorded control score 43.0217308796477 (arm M1, nested_in_domain,
                    Model 2/Model 2 Experiment 3/sweep_results.csv) within 0.05 EMD.
  G1  MAGNITUDE    pooled gain >= 3.00 EMD                    (over the 45 evaluation rows)
  G2  DIRECTION    gain >= 1.00 EMD in EACH direction, and neither direction's clustered 95% CI
                   lies entirely below 0 (no direction may be significantly worse)
  G3  SIGNIFICANCE pooled clustered 95% CI lower bound > 0.
                   Per-direction significance is deliberately NOT required.
  G4  SPECIFICITY  gain(CORAL) - gain(CORAL_INDEP) >= 1.50 EMD AND that difference's clustered CI
                   excludes 0, AND gain(CORAL_INDEP) <= gain(CORAL).
  G5  CANARY      |gain(CORAL_SELF)| <= 0.50 EMD pooled AND <= 0.50 in each direction. Violation =>
                   INVALID-IMPLEMENTATION and NO CONCLUSION AT ALL, including no refutation.
  G6  BOUNDARY    see the boundary rule below - reported per DIRECTION, never pooled, never hidden
                   by the other direction.
  G7  LABEL-BLIND the static and runtime assertions above. Failure stops the run; it is not a
                   result.

  clause test:  G0 AND G1 AND G2 AND G3 AND G4 AND G5 AND G6 AND G7   (all eight must hold)

  SUBMIT = TRUE IF AND ONLY IF VERDICT == SUPPORTED

  The clause conjunction is what the VERDICT is derived from; it is not by itself the submission
  test. G6 fails only when BOTH directions are alpha-clipped, so the conjunction read alone would
  let the one-direction caveat case submit, which the boundary rule and the submission rule below
  both forbid. The statements are therefore ordered: clauses -> verdict -> submit. The notebook
  computes the conjunction, folds in the single-direction caveat, and then asserts
  "SUBMIT == (VERDICT == SUPPORTED)" at run time, so the contract cannot drift from the code in
  silence. Stated explicitly:

    SUPPORTED                      -> submission permitted, provided the 8 structural gates pass
    SUPPORTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT -> NO submission
    REFUTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT   -> NO submission, and no permanent closure
    INCONCLUSIVE                   -> NO submission
    every other verdict            -> NO submission

BOUNDARY-CONVENTION: every threshold above is INCLUSIVE - a value exactly equal to it passes. All
CIs are two-sided percentile intervals from the same 4000 bootstrap draws, resampling UNIT = SOIL
IDENTITY (a soil appearing in both directions moves as one cluster), seed 20260934. Row-level
resampling would treat correlated errors as independent and narrow every CI by roughly sqrt(2),
which is exactly how a null becomes a discovery here.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM - not invented round figures:
  3.00 EMD is (i) about 18% of the +16 to +18 EMD camera penalty that is the explicit target of the
  intervention; (ii) more than three times the 0.9 EMD information-specific effect the curve-ensemble
  spike measured on 2026-09-29 and correctly treated as noise; (iii) larger than any arm difference
  this project has resolved externally - the E1 probe's 0.674 EMD gap was declared a tie, and the
  D-vs-M1 gaps were 0.3 to 1.9 EMD. It is deliberately far below Model 1's 15.0 ORDINAL_FLOOR, which
  was calibrated for ranking ridge alphas over 24 in-domain soils, not for a paired per-soil
  difference with 45 rows.
  1.50 EMD is exactly half the G1 floor, so an arm sitting on the floor cannot be explained by a
  placebo also sitting on the floor.

THE ALPHA-BOUNDARY RULE, PER DIRECTION (spec 5a)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reported for every arm and both directions: the selected alpha, its status FLOOR | INTERIOR |
CEILING, that alpha's own LOGO error, and the grid spread. There is no pooled boundary status. A
symmetric_boundary diagnostic flags when NONE and CORAL hit the same edge; it changes no verdict.

  BOTH directions at a boundary  => INCONCLUSIVE. Neither SUPPORTED nor REFUTED. No submission, and
      no closure claim of any kind: the measured difference is between a transform and a truncation.
  EXACTLY ONE direction at a boundary => the measured verdict stands but is caveated, and
      - SUPPORTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT: NO SUBMISSION. A gate that passed while the grid clipped one
        of its two halves did not pass cleanly. It earns a documented reason to reopen instead.
      - REFUTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT: the permanent closure rule below DOES NOT FIRE, because a
        clipped direction is one where the arm may have been handicapped. Absence of evidence there
        is not evidence of absence.
  Neither caveat can ever produce a submission: SUBMIT is true for SUPPORTED alone, so the caveat
  removes the privilege structurally rather than by convention. Where a caveat and a specific
  failure diagnosis both apply, the precedence block below decides which label is written.
A grid amendment is never applied inside a run. Extending the alpha bounds changes the frozen head
shared with Models 1 and 2 and is a separate owner decision before any rerun.

VERDICT PRECEDENCE - THE TABLE OVERLAPS, SO THE ORDER IS PART OF THE CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two of the rows above can describe the same measurement: a run with exactly one direction
alpha-clipped AND a failing G2 matches both "G2 fails -> DIRECTION-INCONSISTENT" and "one direction
at a boundary with the gate otherwise failing -> REFUTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT". A tie-break is
therefore required, not optional, and it is fixed here before any score exists. The ladder runs top
to bottom and stops at the first match:

  1  NOT (G0 AND G5 AND G7)                 INVALID-IMPLEMENTATION
  2  boundary in BOTH directions            INCONCLUSIVE
  3  G1 AND G2 AND G3 AND G4                SUPPORTED, or SUPPORTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT when
                                            exactly one direction is clipped
  4  G1 AND G2 AND G3 AND NOT G4            REGULARISATION-ARTEFACT
  5  NOT G2                                 DIRECTION-INCONSISTENT
  6  NOT G1 AND G4                          SUPPORTED-BELOW-BAND
  7  G1 AND NOT G3                          NOT-RESOLVED
  8  anything else                          REFUTED, or REFUTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT when exactly
                                            one direction is clipped

Why this order: the spec attaches the caveat label to exactly two outcomes, a PASS with a caveat
(row 3) and a PLAIN REFUTATION with a caveat (row 8), so it is applied only there; a specific
diagnosis (rows 4 to 7) carries more information than a caveat does and is named instead. Validity
precedes every scientific reading, because a broken instrument cannot support any conclusion; and
both-directions clipping precedes all of them, because a grid truncated at both ends makes the
direction and significance tests themselves uninterpretable.

This ordering assigns LABELS only. It moves no threshold, no arm, no clause test and no submission
outcome: SUBMIT remains true for SUPPORTED alone, which row 3 can produce only when nothing is
clipped.

  P6 [BLIND] CORAL_TILE and CORAL_SOIL disagree with the gated arm in magnitude but not in sign. If

      reported as uninterpretable.


SUBMISSION RULE - EXACT AND CONDITIONAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBMIT = TRUE IF AND ONLY IF VERDICT == SUPPORTED. This is the single submission test; the clause
conjunction gates the VERDICT, and this rule gates the FILE. SUPPORTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT does NOT
qualify, nor does REFUTED-WITH-BOUNDARY-CAVEAT, INCONCLUSIVE, or any other verdict. The notebook
asserts the identity rather than relying on the reader to hold two rules straight, so a future edit
that lets SUBMIT be true alongside any other verdict fails the run instead of shipping a file.

  arm CORAL; file Submission_Model5_E1.csv written by the notebook itself under that name, never
  renamed. Configuration is the PRODUCTION recipe, deliberately not the tested one: fit on all 24
  labelled training soils from both cameras, align those 24 soils' image features onto the 35 iPhone
  test-image features with the same CORAL transform, predict the 10 test soils. Alpha comes from the
  same nested procedure on the 24 soils; no alpha may be chosen by looking at the submission. The
  eight structural validation gates run first and must pass.

  DECLARED DIFFERENCE: the submitted model was never itself evaluated by the gate - it uses two more
  fitting soils than either direction, and its alignment target is the same set it predicts. That is
  the legitimate application of a validated recipe, not a measurement, and the record must say so in
  those words.

  THERE IS NO PROBE IN THIS EXPERIMENT. Any other verdict writes no file and names the failed clause.

WHAT THE CONTROLS DID, which is the part worth keeping
   The instrument separated the arms. The same-marginal placebo degraded FURTHER than CORAL in the
   direction that failed (-27.582 vs -18.464 in A), so CORAL and CORAL_INDEP are distinguishable
   and the design detected the effect it was built to detect - in the harmful direction.
   MEAN and CORAL_SELF are EXACT zeros of a different kind: the MEAN arm is a constant shift, the
   soil median commutes with it, and the frozen StandardScaler removes it, so MEAN is structurally
   identical to NONE (verified to 6.9e-15). CORAL_SELF is the identity to 3.7e-15. Consequence:
   P3's CONFIRMED and X1's label are that identity talking, NOT evidence about first moments, and
   G5 is a wiring canary that cannot fire on anything but a wiring fault. The correct reading of
   the covariance half here is that it COST 8.75 EMD.

TWO IMPLEMENTATION DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED BEFORE ANY GATE WAS EVALUATED - see Experiment1.txt
   section 16 for the full account. The first attempt aborted on cell F1's own n_clusters
   assertion. (1) Delta was computed with the sign inverted against this contract, which would have
   reported an 8.75 EMD degradation as an improvement and could have produced SUPPORTED on a
   harmful model. (2) Pooled bootstrap keys were direction-prefixed, so 45 rows became 45 clusters
   and the soil-clustered bootstrap silently became the row bootstrap. Both were corrected to match
   the wording already frozen above; no threshold, arm, seed, precedence, clause or rule moved, and
   the verdict is DIRECTION-INCONSISTENT under all four combinations.
   Correction (2) also REFUTED A PREDICTION IN THIS CONTRACT. It claimed row resampling narrows
   every CI by roughly sqrt(2). Measured on this data the soil-clustered intervals are NARROWER
   (CORAL width 8.81 vs 11.23; CORAL_SOIL 8.35 vs 11.41). The narrowing direction depends on the
   sign of the within-cluster correlation, which was assumed. Row resampling remains invalid here -
   two rows for one soil are not two observations - so the fix stands, but the stated expectation
   about which way it moves the number was wrong.
